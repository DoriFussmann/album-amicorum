# Purpose

This document defines the content, narrative, voice, information hierarchy, and user experience of the Album Amicorum website.

It is the single source of truth for all customer-facing copy, page structure, messaging, and imagery.

This specification intentionally does **not** describe implementation details, frameworks, styling systems, SEO configuration, routing, analytics, checkout logic, or engineering architecture.

Engineering should remain unchanged unless explicitly required to support the experience defined in this document.

# Brand Position

Album Amicorum creates beautifully illustrated Friend Books that become one-of-a-kind keepsakes of childhood.

The company does not sell stationery.

It does not sell journals.

It does not sell activity books.

It creates keepsakes.

Every Friend Book becomes unique because it is filled by the people who matter most.

That transformation—from empty book to irreplaceable keepsake—is the central idea of the brand and should be reinforced throughout the entire website.

---

# Mission

Help children preserve the friendships and memories that shape childhood through beautifully crafted Friend Books designed to be treasured for years to come.

# Brand Principles

Every decision made on the website should reinforce these principles.

## Childhood over Commerce

The website exists to celebrate childhood.

Sales are a consequence—not the purpose—of the experience.

Visitors should never feel sold to.

They should feel welcomed.

---

## Keepsakes over Products

We do not sell books.

We create future heirlooms.

Every sentence should elevate the emotional value of the product above its physical attributes.

---

## Wonder over Excitement

The experience should feel calm.

Curious.

Quiet.

Magical.

Never loud.

Never urgent.

---

## Timeless over Trendy

Nothing on the website should feel temporary.

Avoid trends.

Avoid internet language.

Avoid modern marketing phrases.

The website should still feel appropriate ten years from now.

---

## Publishing over Ecommerce

Album Amicorum should feel like a premium children's publishing house.

Not an online retailer.

The product pages should resemble beautifully presented books—not product listings.

---

## Story before Selling

The visitor should understand:

before

Why they should buy it.

## Purpose

---

## Eyebrow

Our Story

---

## Heading

Inspired by a tradition that began more than 450 years ago.

---

## Body

More than four centuries ago, students traveling across Renaissance Europe carried small books from place to place, inviting friends and teachers to leave handwritten messages, poems, drawings, and signatures along the way.

These books became known as *Album Amicorum*—Latin for "Book of Friends."

That timeless tradition inspired our own.

Today, My Friends Book carries the same spirit into childhood, inviting a new generation to preserve friendships one handwritten page at a time.

## Question

What is My Friends Book?

My Friends Book is a beautifully illustrated Friend Book where friends leave handwritten memories, drawings, favorite things, and messages.

## Question

How does it work?

### Answer

Your child keeps the book and shares it with one friend at a time.

Each friend completes a page in their own way before returning it, allowing the book to grow naturally over weeks, months, or even years.

### Answer

Children enjoy My Friends Book in different ways depending on their age.

Younger children often draw while an adult helps with writing.

Older children usually enjoy completing the pages independently with friends.

# Global Writing Rules

Every page should follow these principles.

Explain before inspiring.

Inspire before selling.

Keep paragraphs short.

Use generous whitespace.

Never over-explain.

Never exaggerate.

Never create urgency.

Never pressure.

Never compare against competitors.

Never use promotional banners.

Never use countdown timers.

Never use scarcity messaging.

Never interrupt the visitor with popups.

The website should always feel calm.

The visitor should feel invited.

Never persuaded.

---

# Final Editorial Principle

Every sentence should answer one question:

"Would this still feel beautiful ten years from now?"

If the answer is no, rewrite it.

Album Amicorum is building keepsakes.

Its website should feel like one.

# Image Standards

## Philosophy

Photography is not decoration.

Photography is storytelling.

Every image should communicate the same feeling as the copy:

quiet

warmth

friendship

wonder

slow childhood

Images should never exist simply to fill space.

Every image should reinforce the narrative.

# Composition

Images should feel observed.

Not staged.

Subjects should rarely look directly into the camera.

Allow negative space.

Use shallow depth of field.

Prefer genuine interaction over smiling at the lens.

---

# Children

Children should appear curious.

Calm.

Thoughtful.

Creative.

Never overly excited.

Never performing for the camera.

Never holding exaggerated expressions.

---

# Parents

Parents should feel present.

Supportive.

Warm.

Their presence should never dominate the frame.
