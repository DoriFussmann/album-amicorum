## Friends from every corner of nature

The Animals edition is painted in a muted, earthy palette — a watercolor gathering of friendly
creatures from all over the natural world. It's warm and unhurried, the kind of world a child returns
to again and again. For the little one who loves every animal they meet, it's a natural thing to share
with friends.

## Open pages to complete

Between the animals, the cream pages are left blank on purpose — room for a friend to write a memory,
add a drawing, name a favorite thing, or leave a message just for one person. Nothing is filled in for
them. Each friend completes their own page, so no two books ever end up alike.

## Kept, not used up

A notebook gets finished and cleared out. This is meant to stay. As it fills, it becomes a record of a
friendship in real handwriting — something that only means more as the years pass.

## For the animal lover

Animals suits a birthday, a class gift, or a present for a new friend settling in. There's nothing to
charge and nothing to swipe — just a pen, a few friends, and time to fill it.
