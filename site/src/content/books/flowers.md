## A garden that keeps growing

The Flowers edition is painted in soft blush and sage — a watercolor garden that feels planted rather
than printed. It's gentle and unhurried, all open petals and quiet green. For the kind, careful child
who notices small things, it's a fitting world to share with friends.

## Space to fill by hand

Between the flowers, the pages are left open on cream paper — room for a friend to write a note, add
a drawing, list a favorite thing, or say something in their own words. None of it is done in advance.
Each friend plants their own page, and no two books ever bloom the same way.

## More than a notebook

A notebook gets used and thrown out. This is made to be kept. As it fills, it stops being blank paper
and becomes a record of a friendship in real handwriting — the kind of thing that only grows more
valuable with time.

## A gift for the gentle ones

Flowers suits spring birthdays, the end of a school year, or a simple thank-you to a good friend. It's
a present with nothing to charge and nothing to log into — just a pen, a few friends, and time to
fill it.
