## Quiet trees and woodland friends

The Forest edition is painted in muted sage and cream — a watercolor woodland of bears, deer, foxes,
and rabbits under a canopy of leaves. It's calm and unhurried, more moss than adventure. For the child
who loves the woods and the animals that live there, it's a world worth sharing with friends.

## Pages left open on purpose

Between the trees, the cream pages are left blank — room for a friend to write a memory, add a drawing,
name a favorite thing, or leave a few words of their own. Nothing is filled in ahead of time. Each
friend completes their own page, so no two books ever grow the same way.

## A keepsake, not a notebook

A notebook gets used and thrown out. This is made to be kept. As it fills, it becomes a record of a
friendship in real handwriting — the kind of thing that only means more as the years pass.

## For the ones who love the woods

Forest suits a birthday, the start of a school year, or a gift for a child who would rather be outside.
There's nothing to charge and nothing to log into — just a pen, a few friends, and time to fill it.
