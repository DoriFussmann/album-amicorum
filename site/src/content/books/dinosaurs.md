## A world long forgotten

The Dinosaurs edition is painted in muted earth tones — a gentle, watercolor version of a prehistoric
world, more wonder than roar. It's adventure at child height: big creatures, big imagination, nothing
frightening. For the kid who wants to know everything about the world that came before, it's a book
worth handing to friends.

## Built to be written in

Across the illustrations, the cream pages are left open — space for a friend to write a memory, draw
something, name a favorite thing, or leave a few words of their own. None of it is pre-printed. What
ends up inside is entirely up to the friends who fill it, which is why every finished book is
different.

## A keepsake that lasts

A notebook gets used up and tossed. This is made to be kept. As friends add to it over a year — or a
whole childhood — it turns into a handwritten record of who was around and what they meant, in their
own hand.

## For the adventurers

Dinosaurs suits birthdays, a big-kid milestone, or the move to a new school where friendships are just
forming. It runs on a pen and some time — nothing to charge, nothing to log into.
