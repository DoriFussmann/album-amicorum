## Stories that wash in with the tide

Painted in dusty blue and cream, the Mermaids edition is the ocean at its gentlest — soft watercolor
waves and the quiet world just beneath them. No storms, just the hush of water and the feeling that
something wonderful is drifting past. For the child who could stay by the sea all day, this is the
book they'll want to pass to their friends.

## Room for real handwriting

The ocean illustrations frame cream pages left deliberately open — space for a friend to write a
memory, draw something, name a favorite thing, or leave a few words only they would say. Nothing is
printed in for them. What the book becomes depends entirely on who picks up the pen.

## It outlasts the notebook drawer

Most things a child fills up get outgrown and cleared away. This one is meant to survive that. As
friends add to it, page by page, it becomes a handwritten record of a particular stretch of childhood
and the people who were in it.

## For the child who loves the water

Mermaids suits summer birthdays, the last day of term, or a goodbye when a friend is moving away —
the moments you want kept rather than scrolled past. All it needs is a pen and a little time.
