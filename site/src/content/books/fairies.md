## A world hidden in the leaves

The Fairies edition is painted in soft sage and blush — a watercolor fairy-garden of tiny wonders
tucked among leaves and flowers. It's the kind of world a child half-believes is real, quiet and full
of small magic. For the little one who lives in their imagination, it's the perfect thing to share
with friends.

## Pages waiting to be filled

Among the fairy illustrations, the cream pages are left open on purpose — room for a friend to write a
memory, draw a picture, name a favorite thing, or leave a message meant only for one person. Nothing
is filled in ahead of time. The book becomes whatever the friends who hold it decide to make it.

## Meant to be kept

Most books a child writes in get outgrown. This one is built to last past that. Handwriting doesn't
age the way a screen does; years later, a friend's actual words on the page are exactly what makes the
book worth having.

## For the dreamers

Fairies suits a quiet birthday, a sleepover, or the start of a new friendship — the small occasions
that deserve something kept by hand. It needs nothing but a pen and a little time.
